Support-ticket classification dataset.
Each classification must use exactly one label from the allowed-label list.
In the Classifications section, write only lines in the form ID | label.
Do not write new ticket text in the Classifications section.

Allowed labels:
- app_crash: An application closes, crashes, freezes, or fails during normal use.
- damaged_item: An item arrived damaged or broken.
- duplicate_charge: The customer was charged more than once for the same purchase or subscription.
- package_not_received: Tracking says delivered, but the customer did not receive the package.
- password_reset_missing: The customer requested a password reset but did not receive the reset email.
- payment_declined: A payment is rejected even though the customer believes the payment method should work.
- refund_pending: The customer is waiting for money to be returned after a cancellation or return.
- tracking_stalled: A shipment is delayed and tracking has not updated.
- unexpected_fee: The customer sees a fee that was not clearly shown or expected.
- verification_phone_old: A sign-in or verification code is being sent to an old or unavailable phone number.
